About AstroDost

Astrology that meets you where you are.

A birth-chart experience on WhatsApp, combining Vedic calculations with clear, AI-assisted explanations.

How it works

AstroDost is an AI-assisted digital astrology service. We use the birth date, time and city you provide to calculate a Vedic birth chart. AI then helps explain chart-based themes and answer follow-up questions in your chosen language.

Clear terms from the start

You receive one free introductory reading. If you want to continue, each paid plan shows its price, question allowance and time limit before purchase. The timer begins with your first paid question.

What AstroDost does not provide

AstroDost does not promise specific outcomes and does not provide medical diagnoses, legal advice, investment recommendations, gambling guidance or emergency support. Customers should use qualified professionals for decisions in those areas.
