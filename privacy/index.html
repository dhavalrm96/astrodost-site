Privacy policy

This policy explains what AstroDost collects and how that information is used.

Information we collect

We may collect your WhatsApp number and profile information, selected language, birth date, birth time, birthplace, messages, chart data, plan selection, service usage, support correspondence and payment references or status. The payment provider handles your payment credentials directly.

How we use information

We use information to calculate your chart, generate and deliver readings, maintain conversation continuity, administer question allowances, verify payment status, provide support, prevent abuse, secure the service and meet legal obligations.

Service providers

We use service providers for WhatsApp messaging, AI generation, geocoding, cloud hosting and payment processing. They process information only as needed to perform those functions and under their own applicable terms and privacy commitments.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction and security records, resolve disputes and comply with legal or accounting requirements. We use access controls and transport security, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete eligible personal information and may stop using the service at any time. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate fraud, dispute or accounting purposes.

Children

AstroDost is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Contact

Send privacy questions or requests through our contact page. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.
